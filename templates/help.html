Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I create an account? Click on the "Sign Up" button and fill out the required fields.
  • What are the password requirements? Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a special character.
  • How do I log in? Enter your username and password on the login page and click "Login".
  • What should I do if I forget my password? Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page.
